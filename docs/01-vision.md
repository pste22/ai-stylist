# 01 — Vision

## One-liner
**A beloved AI character who is your personal stylist** — a voice-first shopping
companion that feels like talking to a stylish friend, not searching a catalog.

## The problem
People don't lack products — they're drowning in them. They lack **confidence, taste
guidance, and joy** in choosing what to wear. Existing tools are utilitarian search
boxes (Amazon Rufus, Google Shopping, Shopify Sidekick). They're fast, but cold.

## The bet
The "AI shopping assistant" space is crowded and utilitarian. The
**emotionally delightful, voice-first, character-driven personal stylist** space is
nearly empty. That gap is our wedge.

## What it is
- A **1:1 voice conversation** with a warm, animated, hand-drawn character.
- It **asks good questions**, understands body, budget, vibe, occasion.
- It **curates 3 great options with reasons** — not 40 results.
- It **remembers you** across sessions (a relationship, not a session).
- It shops **across multiple sources** (retailer-neutral) and hands off to checkout.

## What it is NOT
- Not a faster search engine.
- Not tied to one retailer's inventory.
- Not a sterile corporate chatbot.

## Why we can win
Incumbents are structurally corporate and sterile. They **cannot easily copy taste,
warmth, and character.** Our edge is **emotional design + voice + curation** — exactly
the UX craft the big players lack.

## ⚠️ Brand / legal note
The aesthetic is "Ghibli-*feeling*" (warm, hand-drawn, alive) — but we build an
**original character**. We do **not** use or market the Studio Ghibli name/IP. The
*feeling* is the asset; the trademark is a landmine.

## Winning sentence
> "It's not a faster way to search — it's the feeling of shopping with a stylish friend
> who knows you."
