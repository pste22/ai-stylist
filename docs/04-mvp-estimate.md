# 04 — MVP Ballpark Estimate

> ⚠️ Ballpark only — for a **solo, self-funded developer**. Ranges, not promises.
> Time assumes part-to-full-time focus. Adjust to your real hours.

## Scope of "MVP" here
MVP = **Phase 1 + Phase 2** = a voice-first character that holds a delightful styling
conversation with fake/sample products and feels magical enough to demo to users and
investors. (Real product sourcing = Phase 3, post-MVP.)

---

## ⏱️ Time estimate

| Phase | What | Effort (solo) |
|-------|------|---------------|
| Phase 1 | Latency spike — runnable voice loop | **4–8 weeks** |
| Phase 2 | Persona + avatar + taste → investor demo | **2–3 months** |
| **MVP total** | Phase 1 + 2 | **~3–5 months** |
| Phase 3 | Real multi-source products | +2–3 months |
| Phase 4 | Retention / memory / accounts | +2–3 months |

**Reality check:** The AI layer is the *easy* part. The time sinks are
**real-time low-latency voice**, the **character that feels alive**, and **taste quality**.

---

## 💰 Cost estimate (free-first)

| Stage | Monthly cost |
|-------|--------------|
| Dev / prototype (local models, free tiers) | **~$0** |
| Investor demo (maybe ElevenLabs voice + some GPU) | **~$20–100** |
| Early beta (paid LLM/voice tiers, GPU hosting) | **~$100–500** |

**Where free breaks (pay only here):**
1. **Voice "wow"** — local TTS fine for dev; ElevenLabs for the demo voice.
2. **GPU hosting** — free locally; paid (RunPod/Modal) when serving real users.
3. **Latency at scale** — Groq/Gemini paid tiers after free limits.

---

## 👥 What's needed beyond a solo dev (for a strong raise)
- **Design/animation + brand co-founder** — the character *is* the product. #1 gap.
- **Fashion/styling domain expert** — credibility on taste & curation.
- You = the technical founder. Keep it.

## 🎁 Investor-stage artifacts
1. A 60-second demo that makes people **feel something**.
2. Early traction: waitlist + a few hundred real conversations + return-rate data.
3. Crisp wedge + why-now (voice AI + 2D animation got cheap/good).
4. Unit economics: cost per conversation, click→purchase, commission model.
5. Business model: affiliate commissions → brand partnerships → premium subscription.
