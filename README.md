# 🧵 AI Stylist — Voice-First Personal Shopping Companion

> A warm, hand-drawn AI character who talks to shoppers 1:1 by voice, understands their
> style, and curates clothes for them across multiple sources — like shopping with a
> stylish friend who actually knows you.

## 🎯 Product Principles (North Star)
1. **Delight over breadth** — one magical conversation beats 1,000 products.
2. **Persona is the product** — character, voice, and taste are the moat, not the AI.
3. **De-risk the scary stuff first** — latency and voice quality before features.
